Back + Delts / Chest + Legs, four days a week, in two 8-week blocks with a deload in weeks 8 and 16. Tonnage is counted for every set. Plus Zone 2 walks, steps and the daily neck routine. Every session, step goal and PR loads more plates.

Split: Back + Delts A (Mon), Chest + Legs A (Tue), Back + Delts B (Thu), Chest + Legs B (Fri). Week 1 only: Back + Delts A on Sat 31 Oct and Chest + Legs A on Mon 2 Nov. Weeks run Saturday to Friday.

Progression: double progression. Add reps first. When every set hits the top of the range, add weight next time: about 2 kg per dumbbell, 2.5 kg on cables and machines, 5–10 kg on leg machines. The app flags it with a green ↑ line. RIR = reps in reserve: RIR 1 means one more rep was possible.

Deload early if your main lifts stall or drop two sessions in a row, joints ache, or sleep and motivation crash. In a calorie deficit, holding your strength is already a win.

Exercises: the same every week. Only the deload changes the sets.

Rest: as listed per exercise. If a session runs past 75 min, shorten rest on isolation work first.

Neck and posture routine (daily, 8–10 min, morning):

  1. Chin tucks, 10 × 5–10 s hold. Glide the head straight back, no tilting.
  2. Isometric holds forward, back, left, right: 3 × 10 s each, 30–50% effort, pain-free only.
  3. Scapular retraction and band pull-aparts, 2 × 15. Shoulder blades down and back, neck long.
  4. Wall angels 2 × 10 (prone W-raise from week 8).
  5. Thoracic extension over a chair back or foam roller, 60 s.
  6. Upper-trap and levator stretch, 2 × 30 s each side. Gentle, no fast neck circles.

See a neurologist if you get pain or numbness into the arm, hand weakness, or dizziness or headaches with neck movement.

How tonnage works

Tonnage = weight × reps, added up over every logged set, as in Goncharov's «Тренируйся!». Avg kg per rep = tonnage ÷ reps.

Barbell, Smith, machine, cable stackkg × reps
Dumbbells in both hands (kg = one dumbbell)kg × 2 × reps
One arm at a time (kg and reps per arm)kg × reps × 2 arms
Pull-ups(bodyweight + added kg, or minus assistance) × reps. Bodyweight = your latest weekly check-in, 104 kg until you log one
Hanging knee raiseNot counted

Use it as a trend against your own past weeks. The book aims for +0.5–1 t a week on average over 2–3 months; in a calorie deficit, holding steady is fine. Deload weeks drop on purpose. Machines like the leg press inflate tonnage, so don't compare your numbers with anyone else's.
